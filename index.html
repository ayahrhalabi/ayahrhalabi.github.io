Impacts of Mediterranean and DASH Diets on MPN Symptoms


Myeloproliferative Neoplasms (MPN) is a cancer located within the blood and bone marrow. To address the range of symptoms that occur, 24 MPN patients' symptom scores were gathered over the course of 112 days. The Mediterranean and DASH diet were utilized for their focus on preventing cardiovascular disease, composed of whole grains, fruits, seafood, vegetables, and nuts. We argue that both diets have impact in reducing the symptoms associated with MPN. However, there is not enough evidence to warrant which diet is more influential.


Dynamics of Air Pollution in Lebanon and It’s Impacts


Atmospheric Pollution is caused by the emissions of different sources of greenhouse gases on the earth. These emissions are tiny particles in the atmosphere, known as “aerosols,” of different sizes and volume concentrations. The nature of aerosols varies from season to season and are very dynamic and impact human health and climate. Atmospheric Pollution was analyzed over a country in the Middle East known as Lebanon. Detailed analysis of satellite data have been carried out to study different types of aerosols and their sources, and their impacts on public health, agriculture, and coastal areas. Our aim was to examine which part of Lebanon was more polluted by comparing the different greenhouse gases (carbon monoxide, methane, ozone) and atmospheric parameters (aerosol optical depth - AOD, angstrom exponent - AE). Further, we have analyzed sea temperature and chlorophyll concentrations data available through NASA Giovanni portal to study impacts of airmass along the coast reaching over Lebanon. Long term variability and trends of atmospheric parameters and greenhouse gases and their impacts will be discussed in detail.
